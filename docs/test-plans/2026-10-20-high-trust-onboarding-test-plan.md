## Key Interactions to Verify
1. **PII Redaction & Visualizer:**
   - Type or paste clinical shorthand with patient names ("Sarah Jenkins"), dates of birth ("1992-08-14"), Ontario health card ("1234-567-890-AB"), phone numbers ("416-555-0199"), and email addresses.
   - Verify green highlight chips appear in real-time over detected entities.
   - Click "Preview Anonymized Payload Sent to AI" and verify all entities are substituted with neutral tokens (`[Client Name]`, `[Date]`, `[Phone]`).
   - Trigger generation and verify the received DAP/SOAP note properly incorporates clinical context without any residual PII.

2. **Demo Client ("Jane Doe - Demo"):**
   - New practitioner signs up and verifies "Jane Doe - Demo" is immediately visible on `/clients`.
   - Verify prominent contextual banner: "Demo Client — Test note generation risk-free".
   - Open SessionNoteEditor for Jane Doe, generate note, review auto-locking preview.
   - Click "Purge Demo Data" in Settings / Client list and verify record and related dummy notes are safely removed.

3. **College AI Consent Intake Form:**
   - Open client intake form template.
   - Verify the "AI Clinical Documentation Consent Addendum" checkbox is displayed with plain-language explanation of PHIPA safeguards and data residency.
   - Submitting the form writes `ai_consent_granted = true`, timestamp, and version to Supabase.
   - If unchecked, verify SessionNoteEditor displays an amber notice: "Client has opted out of AI note assistance" and disables the AI assist button.

4. **Audit-Ready Clinical PDF Export:**
   - From SessionNoteEditor, click "Export Official Clinical PDF".
   - Verify PDF renders practitioner registration number, College name, client ID, session date, and the Canadian PHIPA compliance verification seal.

## Edge Cases
- **Canadian French characters / accents:** Ensure names like "Renée Côté" or "François Tremblay" are correctly recognized and scrubbed without encoding corruption.
- **Ambiguous clinical terms:** Ensure clinical acronyms (e.g. "GAD-7", "PHQ-9", "CBT", "MDD", "PTSD") are NOT mistakenly flagged as names or PII.
- **Offline / Network failure:** If Anthropic API or Supabase Edge function is unreachable, ensure user's draft is not lost and helpful retry toast appears.

## Critical Paths & Regression Verification
- Verify that standard note saving and editing for existing non-demo clients remains completely untouched.
- Verify that Stripe subscription webhooks properly upgrade users regardless of whether demo data has been purged.
