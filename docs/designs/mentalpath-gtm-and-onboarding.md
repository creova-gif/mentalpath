## Problem Statement
Solo and small-group Canadian mental health practitioners are struggling with severe "note debt" (spending 5–10 unbillable hours per week drafting clinical session notes). While generic AI tools (ChatGPT, etc.) could accelerate this, therapists are terrified of regulatory sanctions and disciplinary audits from provincial colleges (e.g., CRPO in Ontario, CCPA, BCACC) due to PHIPA/PIPEDA privacy regulations. Incumbents like Jane App ($74–99+/mo) lack native AI note generation and require third-party scribes ($30–60/mo), creating fragmented, expensive tooling.

## Demand Evidence
- **Active pilot usage:** 1–5 beta Canadian therapists are currently testing and using MentalPath in live clinical pilot sessions.
- **Pain manifestation:** Therapists specifically reported feeling overwhelmed by end-of-day note documentation, directly impacting their well-being and clinical capacity.

## Status Quo
- **Current alternative:** Jane App or Owl Practice ($74–99+/mo), augmented by Google Docs, Word templates, and manual e-Transfers.
- **Workaround cost:** Therapists lose 5–10 unbillable hours weekly, pay premium subscription costs for bloated software, and endure ongoing audit anxiety regarding third-party US cloud AI tools.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **Target User:** Solo Canadian registered psychotherapists, clinical counsellors, and social workers operating private practices.
- **Narrowest Wedge:** PHIPA-Safe AI Clinical Note Generator (DAP/SOAP/BIRP/Progress formats) with real-time PII sanitization badges and auto-locking within 24 hours. Eliminates note debt in under 60 seconds per session.

## Constraints
- **PHIPA / PIPEDA Compliance:** Strict Canadian data residency (AWS ca-central-1 / Montreal/Central region via Supabase), zero training on client personal health data, and client-side PII scrubbing prior to model inference.
- **Provincial College Mandates:** Mandatory informed client consent for AI-assisted record keeping, therapist-in-the-loop review/editing prior to note finalization, and immutable audit logs.
- **Pricing ceiling:** Competitive pressure to undercut Jane App's base tier ($74+/mo) while bundling AI notes without nickel-and-diming add-ons.

## Premises
1. **The Wedge:** The AI Note Generator (DAP/SOAP) is the hook that gets therapists in the door, because note debt is their most acute, immediate weekly pain point.
2. **The Trust Moat:** Explicit, visible PHIPA/PII sanitization badges + a 1-click College AI Consent template remove the regulatory fear that currently stops Canadian therapists from adopting AI.
3. **The Pricing Disruption:** Packaging PHIPA AI notes + scheduling + billing at $49 CAD/mo directly undercuts Jane App ($74-99/mo) + external scribes ($30-50/mo) for solo Canadian practitioners.

## Approaches Considered
- **Approach A: The Conversion Polish**
  - Summary: Minimal viable UI tweaks: Add compliance trust badges to note assist, clarify 14-day free trial billing terms on Stripe checkout, and provide a downloadable PDF consent form.
  - Rejected because: Leaves the onboarding cold-start problem unsolved and fails to provide interactive proof of PII scrubbing.
- **Approach B: High-Trust Solo Practice OS (APPROVED)**
  - Summary: End-to-end launch readiness: Real-time client-side PII scrubbing visualizer before Claude API dispatch, 1-click College AI Consent Addendum integrated directly into the client intake portal, interactive dummy client onboarding walkthrough, and self-serve Stripe billing.
- **Approach C: Standalone Note Companion**
  - Summary: Extract the note generator as an embeddable/floating tool that works on top of Jane App.
  - Rejected because: Dilutes the core mission of replacing bloated practice management software with an integrated, modern Canadian solution.

## Recommended Approach: Approach B
Build the "High-Trust Solo Practice OS":
1. **PII Sanitizer & Trust Visualizer:** A client-side review badge before note generation showing names, dates, phone numbers, and identifying details stripped/anonymized before hitting Anthropic APIs.
2. **Integrated College AI Consent Addendum:** Inject an optional, standard CRPO/CCPA-compliant clause into the 7 intake form templates, allowing clients to e-sign informed consent during onboarding.
3. **Interactive Demo Onboarding:** Pre-populate 1 sample client ("Jane Doe - Demo") with sample DAP notes and intake forms so therapists experience the "aha!" moment in < 2 minutes without entering real patient data.
4. **Frictionless 14-Day Solo Trial ($49 CAD/mo):** Stripe Checkout clearly stating "No charge today, cancel anytime in 1-click from settings."

## Success Criteria
- Time to first generated DAP note for new signups: < 3 minutes.
- Conversion rate from 14-day trial to paid $49/mo solo subscription: ≥ 30% among active beta therapists.
- Zero PII transmission in all telemetry and AI request payloads.
