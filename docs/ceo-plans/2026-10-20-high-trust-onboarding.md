# CEO Plan: High-Trust Solo Practice OS & Onboarding

Generated by `/plan-ceo-review` on 2026-10-20
Status: ACTIVE (Scope Expansion Mode)

## Executive Summary
MentalPath is expanding beyond simple practice management to become the undisputed, high-trust Operating System for Canadian mental health practitioners. By turning regulatory compliance into an interactive, visible moat—rather than an invisible backend claim—we dismantle the #1 psychological barrier holding therapists back from adopting AI: fear of College disciplinary audits and PHIPA sanctions.

## Approved Scope (10x Scope Expansion)

### 1. Real-Time Client-Side PII Sanitizer & Trust Visualizer
- **Mechanism:** Client-side regex & entity tokenizer that runs in the browser *before* any text leaves the machine.
- **Visualizer:** Live green highlight chips highlighting scrubbed identifiers (names, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, OHIP/health numbers).
- **Inspection Toggle:** "Preview Anonymized Payload Sent to Claude" button so clinicians can inspect the exact scrubbed prompt.
- **Safety Guarantee:** Zero unredacted PII is transmitted to third-party model endpoints.

### 2. Pre-Loaded Interactive Sample Client ("Jane Doe - Demo")
- **Instant Activation:** New account creation seeds a single demo client record with realistic clinical dummy data (DAP notes, Intake assessment with racialized stress context, sample invoice).
- **Interactive Tour:** Contextual banner allows therapists to test note generation, auto-locking, and PDF exports in under 90 seconds without entering real patient data.
- **1-Click Reset / Purge:** Option to remove the demo client once the therapist is ready to add real clients.

### 3. Integrated College AI Consent Addendum in Client Portal
- **Compliance Standard:** Pre-drafted addendum adhering strictly to CRPO (Ontario), CCPA, and BCACC guidelines regarding AI-assisted documentation.
- **Client E-Signature:** Embedded into the 7 intake form templates with an explicit opt-out toggle that gracefully disables AI drafting for that specific client file without disrupting general care.

### 4. College-Formatted Audit-Ready Clinical PDF Export
- **One-Click Export:** Directly exports finalized DAP/SOAP notes with official practitioner registration number, College affiliation, client ID, session timestamp, and PHIPA compliance seal.
- **External Sharing:** Formatted for direct transmission to referring physicians, insurers (Sun Life, Manulife, Canada Life), and legal requests.

### 5. Visible Trust Badging
- **Intake & Portal Footers:** Clean Canadian trust badges: `🍁 PHIPA & PIPEDA Compliant • Encrypted in Canada (ca-central-1)`.
